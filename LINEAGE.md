# Lineage

The games ASS2ROID: Ex Profundo descends from, or sits beside: in what it plays like, what it is about, and what its interface looks like. For the case study, and for reference while designing.

## Digging downward

- **Motherload** (XGen Studios, 2004). The closest ancestor: drilling down through Mars, a company behind you, fuel and hull to manage on the way down.
- **SteamWorld Dig** (Image & Form, 2013). A mine dug one tile at a time, dark below, a lamp to see by.
- **Dome Keeper** (Bippinbits, 2022). Digging down for resources in a short, repeated run.
- **Dig Dug** (Namco, 1982). The arcade root of digging a tunnel tile by tile.

## Mixing and states

- **Noita** (Nolla Games, 2020). Materials and potions that react in ways you learn by trying; close to the Milk+ menu, the dosage scale and its states.

## Blue-collar space work, corporate humour

- **Hardspace: Shipbreaker** (Blackbird Interactive, 2022). The nearest in tone: debt, a company that owns your time, cheerful paperwork. Our Belt Authority and the Kroeber Foundation live here.
- **Deep Rock Galactic** (Ghost Ship Games, 2020). Mining as a job, with banter.
- **Papers, Please** (Lucas Pope, 2013). Bureaucracy as comedy; the redacted records and the blasting permit that stays pending.

## Panels and instruments

- **Crying Suns** (Alt Shift, 2019). From the moodboard: a command deck of clean panels.
- **Deus Ex: Human Revolution** (Eidos-Montréal, 2011). From the moodboard: amber-and-black interface, geometric and sharp.
- **FTL: Faster Than Light** (Subset Games, 2012). A ship as a panel of systems.
- **Duskers** (Misfits Attic, 2016). Dark derelicts seen only through drone feeds and a terminal; the dark of The Crack.
- **Highfleet** (Konstantin Koshutin, 2021). A physical instrument deck.
- **Objects in Space** (Flat Earth Games, 2019). A flight-deck console as the whole interface.
- **Alien: Isolation** (Creative Assembly, 2014) and **Signalis** (rose-engine, 2022). Retro-futurist screens that belong to the world.

## The radio and its DJs

- **Grand Theft Auto** (Rockstar, from GTA III, 2001). Stations with a character each, hosts talking over the music.
- **Fallout** (Bethesda, from Fallout 3, 2008). A radio host who comments on what you do. Coach Rocky and the Broadcaster come from here.
