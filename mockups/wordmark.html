The wordmark

Three directions. Each shown large, at the sizes the game needs, and on the same start screen over the real rock, so the wordmark is the only thing that changes. All three are Chakra Petch underneath, drawn into a mark rather than typed.

A · InstrumentMachined, like the rest of the interface. The 2 is the one cut-out part: an ink figure on a concrete block, like a keycap or a stamped plate. Calm, and it scales down cleanly.

B · FaultOne cut through the word, and below it the rock has slipped. Ex Profundo, out of the depths: the dig in a single line. The most character, and still one font.

C · DugThe word as a row of tiles in the real rock, each letter on a plate, the 2 as an open seam. The game’s own language; the busiest, and it needs the tiles to read.
