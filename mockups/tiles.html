ASS2ROID · Prototype 4 · design system · group 1

The world: tiles

The interface is machined; the world is raw. Each layer is an abstraction of a real mineral: tiles split into blocks, their tone flowing across the layer, matte. Drawn in code and seeded per tile, so it never flickers. What matters stands out in a fixed order: the prospector, then what glows, then what you collect, then obstacles by their pattern, and plain rock last.

MaterialsOne per layer, from real minerals. Neutral and cool; the descent is felt in the material, not in new colours.

TilesDrawn at 40 px, shown at twice that. Collectibles sit raised on the rock; obstacles are patterns; only glowing things glow.

StatesThe same patch of the Chrome Vein. Frenzy: warm overdrive, tilted. Stillness: cold and drained. And The Crack: dark, then the rave.
