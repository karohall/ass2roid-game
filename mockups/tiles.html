The world: tiles

The rock from the tile lab, with your settings per layer. Every find sits on a plate made the same way: ink, a faint tint, a 1 px border and the symbol, all in the colour of its role. Only the colour changes.

MaterialsYour four settings. Gap 4, block depth 1, tile depth 1 for every layer. All but The Crack dimmed to 72%, so the plates read.

OverlaysOne plate, four roles. Concrete: what you collect. Steel: what you read or work through. Fluo: what is open now. The spore colour: the spores, and nothing else.

Crack SporesFluo now means “open now”, so the spores need a colour of their own. Three, in the dark where it matters.

ProspectorFive suggestions. All have an ink outline, so they hold against light rock. Each one shown in every state the game puts it in.

StatesThe Chrome Vein in Frenzy and Stillness, and The Crack in the dark and in the rave.
