ASS2ROID · Prototype 4 · round 5: control panel feel

Shake and drink keys, as hardware

The Milk+ menu’s button is the one thing in the deck you press most. Three ways to make it a real key, each in the 96 px the menu gives it. Every key carries its number, so the keyboard (1–5, 9, 0) is printed on the hardware.

On the left, every state. On the right, the real Milk+ column, live: click a key, or press 1–5 (and 9, 0) to shake for two seconds, then again to drink.

A · Backlit keycap

A square keycap in a recessed well, the number engraved on it; beside it, a lit legend window that says what pressing does now. The cap sits down and jitters while it shakes; ready, it is lit from below.

B · Annunciator push-button

A cockpit-style lit push-button: one face, two engraved legends, SHAKE above DRINK. The lit half is what pressing does now; shaking fills the SHAKE half in amber, left to right. The number in a small window on the bezel. Both words are always there, so the cycle explains itself.

C · Pilot-lamp button

A round lit push-button in a machined ring, the number on the lens. The ring is the two-second shake, filling amber like a clock; ready, the lens glows. Its word on an engraved plate beside it. The softest of the three; the most like the dosage gauge.

Recommendation

B, the annunciator. It is the only one where both words are always on the key, so a new player sees the whole cycle (shake, then drink) before pressing anything: the onboarding’s “press once to shake, press again to drink” line could go. The amber fill reads as the two-second wait without a separate bar. A is the most tactile but needs the legend to change words; C is the prettiest, but its ring is small at itch.io scale (≈ 31 px). Any of them keeps the number on the key and the row as it is.
