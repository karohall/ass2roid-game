Design system

Gunmetal. Neutral cold steel, a concrete hint, one glow; a pastel for each secret. Every component on this page is built from the tokens in its first three sections and nothing else; the game will be re-skinned from these, keeping its layout.

Chakra Petch · Inter · IBM Plex Sans
4 px grid · one chamfer

RulesOne style per job. Before anything gets a look, ask what it is: does it name a thing, show a value, get pressed, or tell you which key? The answer picks the style, and nothing else may wear it. Two base rules: raised is pressed, sunken is read; cyan is on, amber is attention. No decoration: a bolt, texture or glow needs a reason, and never sits over text. Written 26 Nov; where an older section below disagrees, this one wins.

Realism (27 Nov): one light, from above; three materials, each always the same: matte anodised aluminium for panels, dark glass with a faint reflection for every display (shaft cam, readouts, dial, radio band, log), soft plastic for keys. Details at real scale; labels printed; no outlines: parts are separated by light (a bright top edge, a dark lower edge, recesses shaded at the top). A sleek panel (27 Nov): one fine edge and a light line along the top, the tab shape kept; no rim, tray or bevel. Name plates are quiet lettering with a small dot. ColourMaterials first: a gunmetal deck, aluminium panels, dark glass displays, soft plastic keys, all lit from above. Cyan means a lamp is on, and nothing else. White is lit text. Amber is attention: a warning, wear, the first three hints’ call, never “all is well”. Näsa blue is Näsa only. Concrete has retired: the advisory is glass like every display.

InstrumentsThe dosage meter: the dial is a window cut into the aluminium, its scale behind glass; below the window the panel’s own metal hides the needle’s pivot. The needle is illuminated, not neon. The scale’s words light as you pass them; tip points are amber. The radio: its band behind the same glass, the stations as places, a red needle; lamps beside it are lenses: dark when off, cyan when on and well, a cyan outline when armed, amber for trouble.
