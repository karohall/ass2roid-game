Design system

Gunmetal. Neutral cold steel, a greige hint, one glow. Every component on this page is built from the tokens in its first three sections and nothing else; the game will be re-skinned from these, keeping its layout.

Architype Ballmer · Inter · IBM Plex Sans
4 px grid · one chamfer

ColourSteel does the structure. Greige is the one warm surface. Fluo is the one glow, and it always means: this, now. Näsa blue means Näsa and nothing else.

TypeBallmer for titles and numbers, Inter for anything you read, Plex Sans for the small print. Numbers are tabular, so they don't jitter as they count.
