Vehicle buttons: four directions

Begin shift (amber: the very first step) and Resume (cyan), each on the game’s own plate at real size. Light only for state, cold and monochrome otherwise. Press them: each goes down its own way.

1 · Cars

Engine start button

or press Enter
or press Space

Says: you are turning the rover on. A knurled metal ring, the legend lit, a thin ring of light. Costs: round and compact; longer words (New game, Start digging) get tight.

2 · Trucks and tractors

Rocker switch

or press Enter
or press Space

Says: a row of cab switches. The top half is pressed, a small lit window shows its state; pressing rocks it. Costs: the lower half needs a word (off, paused) or stays blank.

3 · Heavy machinery

Rubber key, backlit

or press Enter
or press Space

Says: made for gloves. Matte, softly domed, the legend glowing through the rubber, an LED dot for “on”. Costs: the softest of the four; rounder than the panels’ cut corners.

4 · Aviation

Split-legend push-button

or press Enter
or press Space

Says: a cockpit annunciator you press. The action on top, lit; the state below. Costs: the most technical and the coldest; close to the radio’s lamps, so pressable and readable look alike.
