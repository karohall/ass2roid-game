ASS2ROID · Prototype 4 · the dosage meter, round 2

A meatier meter, in a cluster

Why the meter looks small now: its arc is so shallow and its radius so short that the dial itself only spans the middle 336 px of the 758 px band, and only 51 px tall. The rest is glass. Two ways out: the same curve, scaled up to fill the band; or a deeper curve (a real dial), which frees the sides for smaller gauges.

The meter can also grow from 160 to 230 px for free: the right-hand column already pushes it 80 px further down than it needs, so the game keeps its scale. The smaller gauges: the shake (two seconds, amber; then lit blue when the bottle is ready) and the dose window (the ×1.5, ×2, ×3 and its 20 seconds, now only a line of text under the word).

A · Full-width sweep

The curve you have, scaled up until it fills the band: the dial is twice as wide and twice as deep, the redlines and the needle thicker, and a blue sweep from the centre to the needle so its position reads from across the room. The two small gauges are short arcs of the same curve in the lower corners.

B · Dial with edgewise strips

A deeper dial, 124° of sweep, on the left, with its own hub; on the right, two vertical edgewise strips, like an aircraft's engine instruments: the shake fills upward, the dose window drains downward. The strips are the “on its side” version.

C · Car cluster

A deep dial in the middle, flanked by two small round gauges like a fuel and a temperature gauge: the shake on the left, the dose window on the right. The most like a dashboard; every gauge the same family.

Recommendation

C, the car cluster. The deeper curve is what makes it meaty: the needle swings visibly with each drink, and the word sits in a real hub. The shake gauge left of the dial reads as “the next drink”, the dose window right of it as “what it will do”, so the cluster tells the drinking story left to right. A is the safest change (same look, bigger), B the most cockpit, but its strips are narrow at itch.io scale. The shake key keeps its own amber fill either way: the key says which bottle, the gauge says how long.
