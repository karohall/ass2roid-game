BorrowsA Garmin-style COM stack: the frequency you’re on, the one you’d swap to, a flip-flop key between. Annunciator lamps above the glareshield.
BetterR shows what it will do before you press it: the next station is always in view. The perk is a lamp (DIG+, MIX+, NV), so you see which station does what at a glance and learn all three.
CostsThe perk becomes an abbreviation; the full sentence moves to the Field Guide. The tagline shrinks to a small grey line next to the song.
