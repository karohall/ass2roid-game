ASS2ROID · Prototype 4 · the dosage meter

The dosage meter, as an instrument

Three directions at the meter’s real width, each recessed behind glass like the other readouts. The same five states for each: balanced, leaning, near the tip with the next drink previewed, and pinned in Frenzy and in Stillness.

A · Sweep gauge

A wide, shallow arc, like an aircraft engine gauge. The needle sweeps; past each tip, a redline. The dosage word sits in the hub, large.

B · Tachometer

A straight graduated scale behind glass, a needle with a lit cap, redline zones at both ends. The dosage word in its own window, like a gear indicator; the dose window beside it.

C · Level meter

A segmented bar lighting outward from the centre, like an audio level meter: cold segments toward Stillness, warm toward Frenzy, a peak marker that lingers. Close kin to the radio.
